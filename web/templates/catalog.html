Catalog guide

Cushion feel

Firm feels more connected to the ground and can feel quicker for intervals, strides, and runners who dislike a soft sink.

Moderate balances protection and response for daily training and uptempo runs.

Plush absorbs more impact for easy miles, long runs, and low-intensity days, but may feel less snappy.

Shoe rotation

  • Easy shoe: cushioned daily trainer for most relaxed mileage.
  • Workout shoe: lighter or firmer tempo shoe for intervals, hills, and progression runs.
  • Race shoe: reserved for key sessions and race day so the foam and plate stay fresh.

Shoe lifespan

Most running shoes last about 300-500 miles. Lightweight racing shoes tend toward the low end; durable daily trainers often last longer.

Replace sooner if the midsole feels flat, the outsole is uneven, or new aches appear. Sources: REI, ASICS.
